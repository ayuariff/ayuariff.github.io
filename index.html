About me

Educator and researcher in information security.

My teaching journey began in 2009 in the Department of Mathematics, Science and Computer at Politeknik Azlan Shah, Perak. In 2011 I moved to the Department of Information and Communication Technology at Politeknik Mersing, Johor, where I spent fourteen years and found my focus in cybersecurity.

What I value most is seeing students grow through practice. With my Cyber Range Academy team, I built CTF teams and organised activities that let students learn security by doing, from internal competitions to awareness programmes. I am also a member of the Malaysia Board of Technologists (MBOT).

Since October 2025 I have been a full-time postgraduate student at the Faculty of Computing, Universiti Teknologi Malaysia. My PhD research is in human-centric threat intelligence.
