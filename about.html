About me

Educator, Professional Technologist and researcher in information security.

My teaching journey began in 2009 in the Department of Mathematics, Science and Computer at Politeknik Azlan Shah, Perak. In 2011 I moved to the Department of Information and Communication Technology at Politeknik Mersing, Johor, where I spent fourteen years and found my focus in cybersecurity.

What I value most is seeing students grow through practice. With my Cyber Range Academy team, I built CTF teams and organised activities that let students learn security by doing, from internal competitions to awareness programmes.

Since October 2025 I have been a full-time PhD student at the Faculty of Computing, Universiti Teknologi Malaysia, sponsored by the {{ site.research.funding }} scholarship. My research focuses on online scam victimization: developing an ontology-driven knowledge graph to support digital consumer protection. Read about my research →
