What this research is about

Online scams cause financial loss and real harm to a growing number of people. Information about how scams work, who they reach and how victims are affected is often spread across many sources, which makes the bigger picture hard to see.

This research develops an ontology of online scam victimization: a shared, structured way of describing scams, the people they affect and how the two are connected. The ontology is then used to build a knowledge graph that links this information together.

Research aim

To develop an ontology-driven knowledge graph of online scam victimization for digital consumer protection.

Why it matters

Once complete, the knowledge graph is intended to help agencies trace the relationships between scams, so they can better understand scam patterns and strengthen digital consumer protection.
